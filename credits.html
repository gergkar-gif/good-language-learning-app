Credits & Licences

Parlour is a free, non-commercial language-learning app for Spanish and Hungarian. This page lists the open linguistic datasets, public-domain literary sources, and operational service providers used by the app.

AI-Assisted Content

Parlour’s curriculum, lessons, grammar explanations, exercises, vocabulary lists, and original stories were co-produced with AI assistance (OpenAI ChatGPT and Anthropic Claude). The project owner directed all design decisions, reviewed content for pedagogical accuracy, and edited material where necessary.

Linguistic Data Sources

  • Spanish Verb Conjugation Data: miko3k/verbos (originally compiled by Fred Jehle, Indiana University–Purdue University Fort Wayne), used for Spanish verb conjugation tables. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (non-commercial use only).
  • Spanish–English Dictionary & Inflection Index: doozan/spanish_data, derived from Spanish Wiktionary, used for word lookup, inflection forms, and frequency ranking. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Hungarian–English Dictionary: kaikki.org machine-readable Wiktionary extracts by Tatu Ylonen (derived from English Wiktionary). Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL.
  • Hungarian Word Frequency Database: petyaracz/hungarian-word-frequencies by Peter Racz (derived from Hungarian Webcorpus 2), used for lemma frequency ranking in good faith for non-commercial educational use (formal licence verification in progress).

Classic Literature & Readings

Simplified classic stories in the Library are adapted from public-domain works (including Miguel de Cervantes, Benito Perez Galdos, Gustavo Adolfo Becquer, Horacio Quiroga, Ruben Dario, Leopoldo Alas, Herman Melville, Jules Verne, Franz Kafka, Louisa May Alcott, Homer, and Aesop). Readings on newer authors are original texts about the author and work.

Service Providers

  • Cloudflare Workers, D1, KV & R2: optional cloud backup, AI grading, fallback word glosses, speech-to-text, and TTS caching.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech: spoken audio synthesis for lessons and stories.
  • Resend & Google Identity Services: optional passwordless email sign-in links and Google Sign-In.
  • GitHub Issues API: optional in-app bug reporting.
