Privacy Policy

Parlour is a personal language-learning app. This page explains what data it collects and why. It's a small, independently run project, not a company — so this policy is short and factual rather than legal boilerplate.

Account and cloud sync (optional)

Signing in is optional and only needed if you want to back up your progress or use it on another device. If you use it:

  • Email sign-in link: you enter an email address, and we send a one-time login link via Resend, our transactional email provider.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose Google Sign-In, Google Identity Services is loaded on demand to verify your account and share your verified email address and first name (the name is saved locally in your browser for the Home greeting).
  • Synced data: once signed in, your learning progress (lesson completion, spaced-repetition review data, XP, level tests, and similar in-app state) and any books you upload into My Texts are stored in Cloudflare D1, a database run by Cloudflare on our behalf.

If you never open sign-in or use the Account feature, no email address, progress data, or Google Sign-In script is sent or loaded — everything is stored locally in your browser.

Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

Requesting an email login link is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, loaded only when you open sign-in, to prevent automated abuse. Turnstile runs in your browser and shares certain signals (such as your IP address and browser characteristics) with Cloudflare to assess whether a request is coming from a bot. See Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Policy Addendum for details of what Turnstile collects and how Cloudflare uses it.

Text-to-speech audio

Spoken audio for lessons, stories, and exercises is generated using Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, called via our own Cloudflare Worker. The text sent for synthesis is app content (words, sentences, story text) — never your email address or personal information. Generated audio is cached locally in your browser so the same text isn't re-synthesized every time.

AI feedback, transcription, and word lookup

Three optional study features use Cloudflare Workers AI via our own Cloudflare Workers when you use them:

  • Writing & speaking grading: when you submit a written or spoken response in open-ended practice or exam prep, the prompt and your response text are sent to Workers AI for CEFR-aligned feedback (falling back to a local, offline grader if unreachable).
  • Mobile voice transcription: on mobile browsers, recorded speaking attempts are sent to Workers AI (Whisper) to transcribe what you said for pronunciation scoring.
  • Fallback word lookup: if you tap a word in the Reader that is missing from the offline dictionary, that word and up to 300 characters of its sentence are sent to Workers AI to generate a suggested gloss, which is cached by word in Cloudflare KV and your browser.

None of these requests include your email address or account identity.

Bug reports (optional)

If you use the flag button to report an error, your current URL, active tab/lesson/step, and any note you choose to write are sent through our Cloudflare Worker to open a public GitHub issue on the project repository. Do not include personal details in a bug report note.

What we don't do

Parlour has no advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts, and does not sell or share your data with anyone beyond the service providers named above, which exist solely to run those features.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your cloud account and all server-stored data at any time:

  • In the app: open Journey → Account and appearance → Delete account and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your email address, synced learning progress, uploaded My Texts books, and magic login links from our Cloudflare D1 database. Local progress stored on your device stays in your browser until you clear site data.
  • Without the app installed: email gergkar@gmail.com from your account’s email address with the subject “Delete Parlour account”, and all server-side data associated with your account will be removed within 7 days.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact gergkar@gmail.com.
