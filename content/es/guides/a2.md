## A2 Targets

- Units: 20
- Lessons: ≈115, with approximately five teaching lessons plus one consolidation/review lesson per unit, matching the finished A1 course structure.
- Core vocabulary: 400 new words across A2 (20 units × ~20 words), bringing the cumulative core vocabulary to approximately 800 words after A1.
- One communicative goal per unit.
- One primary grammar point per unit, with supporting grammar elements drawn directly from the unit's story and developed across its five teaching lessons.
- New vocabulary is capped at approximately 20 words per unit, flat across the level — matching A1's real per-unit pace (Café, Work and Directions all landed around 25 summed across their five lessons) rather than the earlier, unbuilt 40-word target. A2's grammar (participles, object pronouns, indirect questions) already adds more load per unit than A1 ever did; vocabulary is deliberately kept lighter, not heavier, to compensate.
- Earlier grammar and vocabulary are continuously recycled throughout later units rather than being confined to isolated review lessons.
- The grammar progression follows the existing story sequence: the first ten units develop and recycle the pretérito perfecto; Units 11-15 move into the pretérito indefinido and extend its use through object pronouns, temporal links, indirect questions and causal connectors; Unit 16 introduces the futuro próximo while reviewing the indefinido; Units 17-18 consolidate A2; Unit 19 introduces the futuro simple; Unit 20 consolidates the level.
- Original story begins in Unit 1.
- Classic readers begin in Unit 11.
- A2 focuses on becoming an increasingly independent user in everyday life, with reading moving from the controlled original narrative towards adapted texts alongside it.
