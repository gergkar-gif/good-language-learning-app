# Parlour Editorial Style Guide

## General

- Use Peninsular Spanish.
- Use neutral, modern vocabulary.
- Avoid slang unless the lesson specifically introduces it.
- Prefer natural language over textbook language.

---

## Grammar

- One primary grammar point per lesson.
- Maximum 300 words of explanation.
- Maximum 5 worked examples.
- Link to the relevant Lingolia page.
- Do not explain grammar that has not yet been introduced.

---

## Vocabulary

- All core words must come from the canonical Core Lexicon.
- Use lemmas.
- Avoid introducing synonyms unless required.
- Every core word must appear:
  - in the story,
  - in at least one exercise,
  - in the SRS deck.

---

## Stories

- Only use grammar already taught.
- Recycle previously learned vocabulary whenever possible.
- Introduce new vocabulary naturally.
- Keep dialogue realistic.
- Advance the story every lesson.
- No filler scenes.

---

## Exercises

Every exercise should test a learning objective.

Avoid exercises that exist only to increase lesson length.

---

## English

- Use British English throughout.
- Keep translations short and literal where possible.

---

## Formatting

- Sentence case for headings.
- One blank line between sections.
- UTF-8 encoding.
- Markdown only.

---

## Consistency

Characters, locations, lesson order, grammar progression and vocabulary progression must follow the curriculum documents.

If two documents disagree, the curriculum documents take precedence.
