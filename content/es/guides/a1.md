# A1 Curriculum

## Overview

| Lesson | Communicative Goal | Grammar | Vocabulary Theme | Story Event |
|---------|--------------------|----------|------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Introduce yourself | Greetings, ser | Greetings, names, countries | Carlos meets Meg at a Spanish meetup in Hanoi |
| 2 | Exchange personal information | llamarse, tener, question words | Age, languages, nationalities | They get to know each other |
| 3 | Introduce your family | Articles, possessives | Family | They show each other photos of their families |
| 4 | Describe people | Adjective agreement | Appearance, personality, colours | Carlos tells Meg about Ana and her newborn |
| 5 | Describe a home | hay | House, rooms, furniture | Andreas is moving house |
| 6 | Say where things are | estar (location), prepositions | Household objects | Helping Andreas unpack |
| 7 | Talk about your daily routine | Present tense (regular verbs) | Daily routine | A typical weekday |
| 8 | Tell the time and make plans | ir, time expressions | Days, months, time | Planning to meet again |
| 9 | Order food and drinks | gustar, querer | Food and drinks | Dinner together |
| 10 | Go shopping | Demonstratives | Clothes, shopping, money | Buying a gift for Ana's baby |
| 11 | Talk about hobbies | Present tense review | Hobbies and sports | Weekend together |
| 12 | Describe your town | hay review | Places in town | Showing Meg around Hanoi |
| 13 | Ask for and give directions | Prepositions review | Transport and directions | Kaylee visits Hanoi |
| 14 | Talk about the weather | Weather expressions | Weather and seasons | Outdoor plans change |
| 15 | Talk about work and study | hay que, tener que | Work and study | Carlos starts a new job |
| 16 | Talk about health | tener expressions | Body and health | The baby has a check-up |
| 17 | Talk about future plans | ir a + infinitive | Travel and holidays | Planning a family holiday |
| 18 | Review | Consolidation | Mixed | Family celebration |
| 19 | Review | Consolidation | Mixed | Preparing for the holiday |
| 20 | A1 Final Review | Consolidation | Mixed | Holiday begins |

## A1 Targets

- Lessons: 20
- Core vocabulary: 400 words
- One communicative goal per lesson
- One primary grammar point per lesson
- 10 new words in Lesson 1, increasing gradually to ~20
- Original story begins in Lesson 1
- Classic stories begin in Lesson 10
- World texts begin in Lesson 11
