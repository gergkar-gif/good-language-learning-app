# A1 Curriculum

Rewritten 2026-08-07 so this overview matches the story chapters actually written in `content/es/stories/original/a1`, `a1-grammar.md`, `a1-vocabulary-themes.md` and `curriculum.json`.

**This table is the master.** Title and Communicative Goal are copied verbatim
into each lesson's `title` and `goal` fields, and `scripts/audit-lesson.py`
fails a lesson whose file disagrees with the row below. Change a title or goal
*here* first, then sync the lesson file and rerun `python build-manifest.py`.

Title is the topic, never the story — the story has its own name and it lives
in the story file. "Ordering at a Café", not "Café con Meg".

## A1 Targets

- Lessons: 20
- Core vocabulary: 400 words (see `a1-vocabulary-themes.md` for the authoritative per-lesson breakdown; running total currently provisioned to 290)
- One communicative goal per lesson
- One primary grammar point per lesson
- 10 new words in Lesson 1, increasing gradually to ~20
- Original story: complete for all 20 lessons
- Classic stories and world texts: planned from Lesson 10/11 onward, not yet written
