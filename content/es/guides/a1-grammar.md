## A1 Grammar Rules

- One primary grammar point per lesson.
- No past tenses.
- No productive object pronouns.
- No productive reflexive verbs (except fixed expressions if required).
- No subjunctive.
- Grammar explanations should be concise and link to Lingolia for full reference.

---

## A note on recycled and fixed expressions

A few high-frequency expressions (`tener hambre`, `me gusta`) appear in dialogue before their underlying grammar is formally taught, as fixed chunks for recognition rather than production. This is deliberate — it's standard in graded readers — but it means "only use grammar already taught" (see `editorial-style-guide.md`) is enforced at the level of the lesson's *primary, drilled* grammar point, not literally every word in the story. Worth knowing if you're auditing a story against this table and find something that looks early.
