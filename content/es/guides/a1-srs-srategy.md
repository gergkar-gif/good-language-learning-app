# A1 SRS Strategy

## Objective

Reinforce every core vocabulary item through spaced repetition.

---

## Core Principles

- Every new core word is automatically added to the learner's deck.
- Story-only words are **not** added automatically.
- Words are reviewed independently of lessons.
- A word can only exist once in the deck.

---

## Learning Flow

Lesson

↓

Core Vocabulary

↓

Exercises

↓

Story

↓

SRS Deck

↓

Future Reviews

---

## Review Schedule

| Stage | Interval |
|--------|---------:|
| New | Same lesson |
| Review 1 | 1 day |
| Review 2 | 3 days |
| Review 3 | 7 days |
| Review 4 | 14 days |
| Review 5 | 30 days |

Intervals may be adjusted later based on user performance.

---

## Review Types

Vocabulary reviews may use:

- Multiple Choice
- Matching
- Word Builder
- Fill in the Blank

Grammar is reviewed through lesson exercises rather than standalone SRS cards.

---

## Graduation

A word is considered "learned" after the learner successfully completes all scheduled review stages.

Learned words remain eligible for occasional review.

---

## Lesson Requirements

Every core vocabulary item must:

- appear in the vocabulary list.
- appear in the original story.
- appear in at least one exercise.
- enter the SRS deck.

No exceptions.
