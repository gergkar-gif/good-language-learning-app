# A1 Learning Objectives

Regenerated 2026-09-17 from `content/es/curriculum/units/a1.json` and the
real lesson files — this replaced an older 20-lesson doc, as A1 is now
**26 units × 6 lessons each** (156 lessons total, see `a1.md` for the
full curriculum overview).

## Unit 1 — Greetings & Introductions

### Lesson 01

**Goal**
Greet someone and respond appropriately in simple Spanish.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- greet someone in Spanish.
- respond to a basic greeting.

---

### Lesson 02

**Goal**
Say your name and introduce yourself with a simple sentence using ser.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- say my name in Spanish.
- use soy to say who I am.

---

### Lesson 03

**Goal**
Ask someone's name and understand a simple answer.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- ask someone their name.
- understand and give a simple answer about my name.

---

### Lesson 04

**Goal**
Say where you are from and ask another person about their origin.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- say where I am from.
- ask where another person is from.

---

### Lesson 05

**Goal**
Have a short first conversation using greetings, names and origin.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- have a short first conversation.
- introduce myself and say where I am from.

---

### Consolidation

**Goal**
Consolidate greetings, names, ser, origin and basic questions from Unit 1.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- greet someone and respond appropriately.
- ask and answer about names and origin.
- combine these patterns in a short conversation.

## Unit 7 — Daily Routine: Reflexive Verbs

### Lesson 01

**Goal**
Introduce reflexive pronouns and routine verbs (levantarse, ducharse, lavarse).

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- say what time I get up and shower using reflexive pronouns.

---

### Lesson 02

**Goal**
Master stem-changing reflexive verbs (despertarse [e->ie], acostarse [o->ue], sentarse [e->ie]).

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- conjugate despertarse and acostarse with the correct vowel changes.

---

### Lesson 03

**Goal**
Learn verbs for dressing and grooming: vestirse (e->i), ponerse (yo me pongo), quitarse, peinarse.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- describe what I put on and wear each morning.

---

### Lesson 04

**Goal**
Contrast reflexive actions done to oneself vs transitive actions done to other people or objects.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- i understand when to use a reflexive pronoun and when not to.

---

### Lesson 05

**Goal**
Sequence daily routine actions with temporal connectors (primero, luego, después, antes de, por último).

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- narrate my full day step-by-step using sequence connectors.

---

### Consolidation

**Goal**
Review all reflexive pronouns, stem changes, grooming verbs, and daily routine sequencing.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- describe my entire morning and evening routine naturally using reflexive verbs.
