# A1 Learning Objectives

## Lesson 1

**Goal**
Introduce yourself.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- greet someone.
- say their name.
- ask another person's name.
- say where they are from.

---

## Lesson 2

**Goal**
Exchange personal information.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- ask someone's age.
- say their age.
- say which languages they speak.
- say their nationality.

---

## Lesson 3

**Goal**
Talk about family.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- introduce family members.
- describe simple family relationships.
- ask about someone else's family.

---

## Lesson 4

**Goal**
Describe people.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- describe someone's appearance.
- describe someone's personality.
- describe basic colours.

---

## Lesson 5

**Goal**
Describe a home.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- describe a house.
- name rooms.
- say what exists in a room.

---

## Lesson 6

**Goal**
Say where things are.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- describe the location of objects.
- use basic location prepositions.

---

## Lesson 7

**Goal**
Talk about daily routines.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- describe a typical day.
- talk about common daily activities.

---

## Lesson 8

**Goal**
Arrange to meet someone.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- tell the time.
- mention dates.
- arrange a meeting.

---

## Lesson 9

**Goal**
Order food and drinks.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- order from a menu.
- say what they like.
- ask for food and drinks.

---

## Lesson 10

**Goal**
Go shopping.

**By the end of this lesson, the learner can:**

- ask about prices.
- buy clothes.
- use demonstratives naturally.

---

**Goal**
