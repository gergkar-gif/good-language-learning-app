# A1 Content Specification

This document defines the minimum content required for every A1 lesson.

---

## 1. Grammar

- 1 grammar explanation
- Maximum 300 words
- 3–5 worked examples
- 1 Lingolia reference

---

## 2. Core Vocabulary

- Lesson 1–2: 10 words
- Lesson 3–4: 15 words
- Lesson 5–8: 20 words
- Lesson 9–17: 20–30 words
- Lesson 18–20: Review only

Every word must come from the Core Lexicon.

---

## 3. Original Story

Required.

Target length:

| Lessons | Words |
|---------|------:|
| 1–3 | 100–150 |
| 4–8 | 150–250 |
| 9–14 | 250–400 |
| 15–20 | 400–600 |

Only previously introduced grammar may be used.

---

## 4. Exercises

Exactly 9 exercises.

| Type | Count |
|------|------:|
| Vocabulary | 2 |
| Grammar | 2 |
| Reading | 2 |
| Dialogue | 2 |
| Writing | 1 |

---

## 5. Classic Story

| Lessons | Required |
|---------|----------|
| 1–9 | No |
| 10 | Yes |
| 11–20 | Yes |

Target length:

100–300 words.

---

## 6. World Text

| Lessons | Required |
|---------|----------|
| 1–10 | No |
| 11–20 | Yes |

Target length:

75–200 words.

---

## 7. SRS

Every core vocabulary item is automatically added to the learner's SRS deck.

---

## 8. Can-do Checklist

Exactly four checklist items.
