# B1 Structural Summary

### Core Spanish
- 36 units
- 6 lessons per unit (5 teaching lessons + 1 consolidation lesson)
- 216 principal lessons
- approximately 576 new lexical items
- literary, historical and world texts replace original Parlour stories
- progressively more authentic reading
- increasing connected discourse and production
- cumulative grammar and vocabulary retrieval

### Latin America
- 36 units
- 6 lessons per unit (5 teaching lessons + 1 consolidation lesson)
- 216 principal lessons
- approximately 576 new lexical/content items
- historical progression from pre-Columbian societies to approximately 2000
- Spanish-language historical, literary, testimonial and documentary texts
- progressively greater emphasis on interpretation rather than factual recall

### Core textual progression

**Early B1:** adapted classics and accessible narratives  
**Mid B1:** adapted literary, philosophical and social texts  
**Late B1:** increasingly authentic literary, historical and argumentative texts

The text is no longer an optional Story component. It is part of the **core language-learning material** for the unit.
