# Hungarian A2 story arc (planning doc)

Companion to `content/hu/a2-curriculum-draft.json`. That file plans the
grammar/vocab/theme scaffolding for all 30 A2 units; this plans the
*story* that runs across the same 30 units' readings, so each unit's
reading is both a fresh scene matching that unit's grammar/vocab AND one
beat in a single coherent arc — not 30 unrelated vignettes.

## Canon (established in A1, `content/hu/stories/original/a1/*.json`)

- **Meg** — the learner. South African, from Johannesburg. Moved to
  Budapest to live with her boyfriend Károly. Has twin sisters, **Kaylee**
  and **Lauren**, still in Johannesburg.
- **Károly** — Meg's boyfriend, lives with her in Budapest.
- **Anna** — Károly's older sister. Has a baby (~1 year old as of A1).
- **András** — Károly's younger brother. A schoolteacher.
- **Mariann** — mutual friend and Meg's Hungarian tutor.
- A1 ends (Unit 30) with the whole group making weekend plans together —
  Meg is settled, has a social circle, and is functionally conversational.

## The A2 throughline

A2 is about Meg going from "settled and conversational" to "actually
building an independent life in Hungary" — job, home, friends of her own,
bureaucracy handled solo, cultural fluency, not just linguistic fluency.
The throughline has a real causal spine, not just a checklist of
milestones: Károly proposes at Unit 26 ("Future Plans"), which is what
motivates her, at Units 29-30, to actually look into what marrying a
Hungarian citizen means for her legal status and decide to start
studying for the citizenship exam. The exam is a practical/relationship-
driven decision, not an identity one — **Meg stays South African at
heart throughout**; nothing in this arc has her "choosing" Hungary over
where she's from (see Unit 28).

## Unit-by-unit plot

1. **Daily Life & Routines** — *(built 2026-08-29)* Steady job, established
   routine — she's settled into everyday life.
2. **Time, Dates & Schedules** — Juggling a real calendar: work, a
   Hungarian class, the dentist. Scheduled like a local now, not just
   surviving day to day.
3. **Family & Family Life** — First proper visit with Károly's parents
   (new characters). Meeting the in-laws is a real integration milestone;
   she compares family customs to her own back home.
4. **People & Personality** — Describes new colleagues and a neighbor
   she's gotten to know. Her social world is expanding past the core
   group.
5. **Friends & Relationships** — Makes a friend independently of Károly
   (an expat or a work colleague). She has her own life now, not just his.
6. **Home & Housing** — She and Károly move into their own flat together.
   First home as a couple — a concrete "putting down roots" beat.
7. **Neighbourhood & City** — Settling into the new neighborhood —
   favorite bakery, the park, knows her way around without help.
8. **Shopping & Prices** — Confidently haggling/comparing prices at the
   market. No longer the confused newcomer.
9. **Food & Eating Habits** — Adopts Hungarian meal habits (Sunday family
   lunch) as part of her own routine now.
10. **Cooking** — Károly's mother teaches her a family recipe (gulyás or
    similar). Cultural absorption, not just tourism.
11. **Leisure & Hobbies** — Joins a local hobby group/class. Building a
    life, not just a routine.
12. **Culture & Going Out** — A night out at a concert/film with the whole
    group. Cultural fluency, not just linguistic.
13. **Weather & Seasons** — Her first Hungarian Christmas/winter with the
    family.
14. **Transport & Getting Around** — Commutes and navigates the city like
    a local; maybe helps a lost tourist with directions (a nice
    reversal-of-role beat).
15. **Travel & Holidays** — A weekend trip within Hungary with Károly —
    exploring the country as home turf, not as a visitor.
16. **Hotels & Accommodation** — Continues the trip; handles a booking
    problem herself.
17. **Health & the Body** — Navigates the Hungarian healthcare system
    alone (a doctor's visit) — a real independence milestone.
18. **Healthy Living & Advice** — Anna gives her (unsolicited) health
    advice — sibling-in-law dynamic deepens.
19. **School & Language Learning** — Reflects explicitly on her own
    Hungarian-learning journey; maybe helps a newer expat friend get
    started. Plants "language learning as an ongoing project."
20. **Work & Professions** — Her job stabilizes — a raise or new
    responsibility. Building a career in Hungary, not just passing
    through.
21. **Verb Prefixes** — An errands day (in and out of a dozen places).
    Grammar-driven theme, low-plot "logistics of daily life" unit.
22. **Ability, Possibility & Permission** — Renews her residence permit at
    the immigration office. First real brush with Hungarian bureaucracy,
    handled solo.
23. **Talking About the Past I** — The one-year anniversary of her move —
    she looks back on how far she's come.
24. **Talking About the Past II** — The group swaps stories from that
    first year (a funny early mistake of hers, retold with affection).
25. **Telling Stories** — A storytelling evening with friends — she tells
    a story fluently, unprompted. A fluency milestone.
26. **Future Plans** — **Károly proposes.** They talk future plans as a
    real "us" — the emotional turning point of the level.
27. **Suggestions & Conditional** — The group plans the engagement
    celebration together.
28. **Opinions, Preferences & Comparisons** — Now engaged, she compares
    life in Hungary and South Africa honestly — what she loves and misses
    about each — and lands somewhere truer: **she doesn't have to
    choose.** She's building a life in Hungary without becoming any less
    South African.
29. **Problems, Requests & Everyday Communication** — Solves a real
    bureaucratic problem alone at an office, and — prompted by the
    upcoming marriage — asks about what marrying a Hungarian citizen
    means for her status.
30. **Living in Hungarian** — Capstone: she decides to start studying for
    the citizenship exam. Explicit narrative handoff into B1/B2's
    dual-track (see `ROADMAP.md`'s "Hungarian: introduce Hungarian
    cultural material at B1-B2" item).

## Review units (31-33)

No new plot — these are grammar/communication review, not narrative
beats. If they get readings at all, they should recap/consolidate
rather than advance the story (matching how A1's consolidation lessons
work).
