- 01 NEW `derivacion-nominal-verbal`, screen `c1-03-01-a-gr`: how specialised Spanish builds terms from a root. Nouns from verbs and adjectives (*-ción/-sión*, *-miento*, *-dad/-idad*, *-ización*, *-ura*: *fermentar → fermentación*, *crecer → crecimiento*, *estable → estabilidad*); verbs from nouns and adjectives (*-izar*, *-ificar*, *-ecer*: *estabilizar*, *purificar*, *oscurecer*); verbs made on both sides at once (*en-…-ecer*, *a-…-ar*: *enriquecer*, *abaratar*, *ensanchar*). Why: a reader who knows the root and the suffix can decode most technical words. A table per pattern; tip: English *-tion* maps to *-ción* often but not always, and the *-izar* verbs are not all valid (never invent a derived word: use only words a dictionary lists). At least 6 items tagged with it; a "which word is formed correctly" item must have only real Spanish words as its right answer and a clearly non-existent or wrong-suffix form as each wrong option.
