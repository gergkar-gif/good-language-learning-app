Genre: exhibition review
This unit teaches no new skill: every lesson recaps known B1/B2 skills at C1 density in visual arts, curation, and art criticism (screens open with "You know *X* from <Level> Unit N", the unit number taken from the knowledge sheet, and add what is new at C1: how exhibition reviews and critical prose use the form). Items are tagged with those skills or `c1-art-criticism-vocab`.
Lesson plan:
- 01 delivering considered aesthetic judgements: recap `resultar-adjective` (B1 Unit 15) — extending B1 Unit 15's *resultar* + adjective (*el argumento resulta convincente*) to critical prose: *resultar* + adjective + *de* + infinitive (*la pieza resulta difícil de clasificar*), impersonal *resultar* + adjective + infinitive (*resulta imposible pasar por alto el claroscuro*), and *resultar* + indirect object (*al espectador le resulta desconcertante*).
- 02 describing composition, staging, and curatorial design: recap `pasiva-estado-estar-participio` (B2 Unit 20) — extending B2 Unit 20's resultative *estar* + participle (*la puerta está cerrada*) to spatial layout and artistic design (*la muestra está articulada en tres salas*, *los bocetos están dispuestos en semicírculo*, *el lienzo está resuelto con trazos sobrios*), contrasting current curatorial state (*estar*) with historical creation or restoration (*fue tallado*, *fue restaurado por el taller*).
- 03 calibrating praise and critical superlatives: recap `superlatives` (B1 Unit 7) — extending B1 Unit 7's *el/la más* + adjective and regular *-ísimo* to critical restraint and precision: relative superlatives followed by *que* + subjunctive (*la retrospectiva más ambiciosa que haya acogido el museo*), spelling-change and formal superlatives (*riquísimo*, *amplísimo*), and *sumamente* + adjective as a measured critical alternative to *-ísimo*.
- 04 analysing technique and viewing perspective: recap `manner-clauses` (B2 Unit 32) — extending B2 Unit 32's *como* and *según* + verb to art criticism: *tal como* + indicative to cite curatorial framing or visual detail (*tal como señala el catálogo*), *según* + subjunctive for shifting viewing conditions (*según se mire desde la entrada o desde el fondo*), and *el modo en que* / *la manera en que* to examine artistic execution.
- 05 writing with an impersonal critical gaze: recap `se-impersonal` (B1 Unit 6) — extending B1 Unit 6's workplace *se* to exhibition reviews: impersonal *se* with intransitive and prepositional verbs (*desde el patio se accede a la galería*, *en esta serie se dialoga con la tradición*) and invariable singular *se* before human *a*-objects (*se invita al visitante a detenerse*, *se homenajea a las artesanas*, never plural); plus the unit exhibition review and the Reading group.
Domain words: visual arts, curation, and art criticism (*la muestra, la retrospectiva, la comisaria / el comisario, la curaduría, el lienzo, el boceto, la pincelada, el trazo, el claroscuro, la paleta, cromático, la textura, la escultura, la instalación, el grabado, el relieve, el encuadre, la perspectiva, la vanguardia, figurativo, abstracto, minimalista, evocador, sobrio, desconcertante, audaz, el catálogo, la galería, el visitante, el espectador*); verify each against `.scratch/sheet-art-criticism.md` before writing so no earlier lemma is re-listed.
Text: an exhibition review of an invented retrospective at a municipal museum of contemporary art—examining how the curatorial layout moves from early charcoal sketches to large-scale textile and ceramic installations, praising the lighting and spatial rhythm while noting an uneven final room. General, uncontroversial aesthetic observations hedged where they are judgements; no real artist, curator, museum, artwork, quotation, date, or statistic.
