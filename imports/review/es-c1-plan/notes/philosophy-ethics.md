Genre: dialogue essay
This unit teaches no new skill: every lesson recaps known B1/B2 skills at C1 density in philosophy, logic, and ethics (screens open with "You know *X* from <Level> Unit N", the unit number taken from the knowledge sheet, and add what is new at C1: how philosophical dialogue and ethical essays use the form). Items are tagged with those skills or `c1-philosophy-ethics-vocab`.
Lesson plan:
- 01 naming abstract ethical concepts with neuter *lo*: recap `lo-neutro-abstraccion` (B1 Unit 40) — extending B1 Unit 40's everyday *lo bueno*, *lo malo*, *lo importante* to philosophical abstraction: moral and logical concepts (*lo justo*, *lo verdadero*, *lo universal* frente a *lo particular*), *lo* + past participle (*lo debido*, *lo permitido*, *lo acordado*), and *lo* + adjective + *de* + noun (*lo paradójico del dilema*).
- 02 separating moral duty from epistemic inference: recap `perifrasis-deber-obligacion-conjetura` (B2 Unit 26) — extending B2 Unit 26's present *deber* vs *deber de* to past ethical evaluation (*debió actuar con prudencia* for duty vs *debió de actuar por miedo* for inference) and impersonal moral principles (*se debe distinguir entre el fin y los medios*).
- 03 framing thought experiments and universal principles: recap `si-clauses` (B1 Unit 8) — extending B1 Unit 8's realistic and hypothetical *si* clauses to philosophical reasoning: universal gnomic conditionals (*si una norma perjudica al vulnerable, carece de legitimidad*) and mixed conditionals linking a timeless condition to a past result (*si no existiera la confianza, ningún pacto habría sobrevivido*).
- 04 judging moral responsibility and avoidable harm: recap `regrets-reproaches` (B2 Unit 10) — extending B2 Unit 10's personal *debería haber* to third-person ethical assessment of moral agency: contrasting *debería haber* + participle (unfulfilled moral obligation) with *podría haber* + participle (unused ethical alternative) and *no tenía por qué* + infinitive ('had no reason to').
- 05 building deductive chains in philosophical dialogue: recap `consequence-markers` (B2 Unit 31) — extending B2 Unit 31's *por lo tanto*, *por consiguiente*, and *en consecuencia* to philosophical deduction: *de ahí que* + subjunctive ('hence / that is why'), deductive *luego* ('therefore' in logical argument), and *de modo que* + subjunctive for normative purpose vs indicative for result; plus the unit dialogue essay and the Reading group.
Domain words: philosophy, logic, and ethics (*la ética, el dilema, la premisa, la conclusión, la validez, el razonamiento, la virtud, el deber, la autonomía, la conciencia, la prudencia, la coherencia, la contradicción, el criterio, el principio, la finalidad, el bien, el mal, lo justo, racional, legítimo, ético, moral, universal, particular, imparcial, deliberar, justificar, deducir, refutar*); verify each against `.scratch/sheet-philosophy-ethics.md` before writing so no earlier lemma is re-listed.
Text: a philosophical dialogue essay between two unnamed interlocutors debating whether the moral worth of an action rests on duty and keeping one's word or on weighing its consequences, tested through an invented everyday dilemma about a promise. General, uncontroversial ethical arguments hedged where they are judgements; no real philosopher named, no quotation, no real work, date, or statistic.
