Genre: report
This unit teaches no new skill: every lesson recaps known B1/B2 skills at C1 density in human rights, justice, and institutional oversight (screens open with "You know *X* from <Level> Unit N", the unit number taken from the knowledge sheet, and add what is new at C1: how human-rights and judicial reports use the form). Items are tagged with those skills or `c1-human-rights-vocab`.
Lesson plan:
- 01 reporting institutional obligations and violations: recap `ser-passive` (B1 Unit 18) — extending B1 Unit 18's simple preterite passive (*fue aprobada por*) to compound and modal passives in human-rights reports (*han sido vulnerados*, *había sido ratificada*, *deben ser garantizadas por tribunales imparciales*, *pueden ser revisados por una instancia superior*), keeping gender/number agreement on the final participle while *sido* stays invariable.
- 02 stating protective mandates and legal aims: recap `purpose-connectors` (B2 Unit 12) — extending B2 Unit 12's *con miras a*, *con vistas a*, *con el fin de* (+ infinitive), and *de modo que* (+ subjunctive) to formal report purpose clauses with subject change (*a fin de que*, *con el objeto de que*, *con el fin de que* + subjunctive) contrasted with same-subject *a fin de* / *con el objeto de* + infinitive.
- 03 stating universal protections regardless of circumstances: recap `concesivas-aun-cuando` (B2 Unit 8) — extending B2 Unit 8's *aun cuando* to legal and human-rights scope: pairing *aun cuando* with the imperfect subjunctive for hypothetical guarantees (*aun cuando no existiera una denuncia formal*) and with the present perfect subjunctive for conceded-as-possible prior events (*aun cuando el plazo haya vencido*), contrasting unaccented *aun* ('even') with accented *aún* ('still').
- 04 evaluating compliance and pending remedies: recap `subjuntivo-perfecto` (B1 Unit 37) — extending B1 Unit 37's present perfect subjunctive beyond personal feelings to institutional monitoring and oversight: passive perfect subjunctive (*lamentan que no se haya respetado la presunción de inocencia*, *celebran que las medidas hayan sido aplicadas*) and anterior-to-future deadlines (*antes de que se haya resuelto la apelación*).
- 05 reporting commission inquiries and findings: recap `discurso-indirecto-interrogativas` (B2 Unit 17) — extending B2 Unit 17's reported questions (*preguntar si*, *querer saber*) to formal investigative and judicial reporting verbs (*indagar si*, *examinar cómo*, *determinar quién*, *verificar por qué*) and preposition + interrogative frames (*examinó en qué medida*, *indagó a quién correspondía la reparación*); plus the unit report and the Reading group.
Domain words: human rights, justice, and oversight (*el debido proceso, vulnerar, la vulneración, la ratificación, la imparcialidad, imparcial, la salvaguardia, la defensoría, el amparo, el acceso a la justicia, la igualdad ante la ley, la tutela, la arbitrariedad, arbitrario, la denuncia, la indefensión, irrenunciable, la libertad de expresión, la apelación, la reparación, la restitución, el fallo, el cumplimiento, la presunción de inocencia, indagar, la indagación, el perito, la jurisdicción, la recomendación, el seguimiento*); verify each against `.scratch/sheet-human-rights.md` before writing so no earlier lemma is re-listed.
Text: an annual oversight report from an invented municipal ombudsman office (*defensoría*) reviewing access to justice, due process, impartial hearings, and follow-up on citizen complaints in an invented regional jurisdiction—comparing formal legal safeguards with everyday barriers faced by residents, and recommending clearer remedies and monitoring. General, uncontroversial principles hedged where they are judgements; no real person, country, government, party, law quotation, date, or statistic.
