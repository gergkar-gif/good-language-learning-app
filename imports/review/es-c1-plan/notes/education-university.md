Genre: essay on education
Lesson plan:
- 01 framing empirical findings and institutional debates in higher education: recap `el-hecho-de-que-subjuntivo` (B2 Unit 6) — thematic *el hecho de que* + subjunctive (*no implica que*, *exige que*, *pone de relieve que*) in university policy and research evaluation.
- 02 academic obligations, syllabus requirements, and historical milestones: recap `perifrasis-haber-de-infinitivo` (B2 Unit 26) — formal deontic *haber de* + infinitive (*los doctorandos han de defender*, *la universidad ha de garantizar*) and prospective-in-past *había de / habría de* + infinitive in institutional history.
- 03 passive and impersonal structures in university governance and peer review: recap `choosing-passive-type` (B2 Unit 20) — contrasting *se* passive/impersonal (*se otorgaron las becas*), process *ser* passive with an explicit academic body (*la tesis fue evaluada por el tribunal*), and resultant state *estar* + participle (*el plan de estudios ya está aprobado*).
- 04 parenthetical commentary and digression in lectures and seminars: recap `marcadores-digresion` (B2 Unit 28) — *por cierto*, *a propósito de*, *dicho sea de paso*, and *volviendo a lo que decíamos* to weave incidental academic clarifications into a seminar without losing the main thread.
- 05 essay structure, absolute participle clauses, and academic synthesis: recap `essay-style` (B2 Unit 35) — nominalised topic openings, absolute participle clauses (*aprobado el plan*, *defendida la tesis*), *si bien es cierto que*, and formal conclusions; plus the unit text and the Reading group.
Domain words: higher education and university governance (*la cátedra, el rector, el decano, la facultad, el posgrado, la licenciatura, el doctorado, la tesis, el tribunal, la beca, la matrícula, el plan de estudios, el claustro, el seminario, la docencia, acreditado, presencial, impartir, matricularse, otorgar*); check each against the sheet.
Text: an essay on the mission of the public and research university in the twenty-first century—balancing open access, classroom teaching, and long-term scientific inquiry against short-term vocational pressures; no invented statistics, no named real people.
