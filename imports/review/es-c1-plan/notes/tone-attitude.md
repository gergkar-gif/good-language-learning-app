Genre: crónica
Lesson plan:
- 01 NEW `sufijos-apreciativos`, screen `c1-22-01-a-gr`: suffixes that add feeling, not size alone. Diminutives *-ito/-ita* (all areas), *-illo/-illa*, *-ico* (Caribbean, Colombia, Costa Rica, and parts of Spain), for affection, politeness or playing down: *un momentito*, *un cafecito*, *ahorita* (Mexico and Central America: "right now / in a while"). Augmentatives *-ón/-ona*, *-azo/-aza* (also "a blow with": *un portazo*, *un codazo*) for size or admiration: *un golazo*. Pejoratives *-ucho/-ucha*, *-ejo*: *un cuartucho*, *un pueblucho*. You know superlatives in *-ísimo* from B1 Unit 7: recap them. Say that the same suffix can be warm or ironic depending on context, and that diminutive use is higher in much of Latin America. Items use only real, common derived forms (never invent one) and pin the attitude in the prompt. At least 6 items tagged with it.
- 02 politeness by playing things down: recap `imperfecto-de-cortesia` (B2) with diminutives (*¿Me esperas un momentito?*).
- 03 intensifying: recap `superlatives` (B1) and `resultar-adjective` (B1).
- 04 describing places with attitude: recap `relative-clauses` (B1) at C1 density.
- 05 the voice of a *crónica*: recap `stance-adverbs` (B2); plus the text and the Reading group.
Domain words: places and attitude (*el barrio* only if not listed, *la plaza* only if not listed, *el portazo, el cuartucho, entrañable, cursi, despectivo*); check each against the sheet.
Text: a *crónica* (a personal, literary report) of a Saturday market in an invented Latin American town, warm and a little ironic, using diminutives and augmentatives naturally. No real place or person.
