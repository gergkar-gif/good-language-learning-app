Genre: satirical column
Lesson plan:
- 01 NEW `ni-que-exclamativo`, screen `c1-21-01-a-gr`: *¡Ni que* + imperfect (or pluperfect) subjunctive! = an ironic "as if…!" rejecting an assumption: *¡Ni que fuera millonario!* (he spends as if he were), *¡Ni que hubieras visto un fantasma!*. You know *como si* + subjunctive from B2 Unit 15: recap it; *ni que* is the exclamative, ironic version and stands alone. Imperfect or pluperfect subjunctive only (*ni que es* is wrong). At least 6 items tagged with it.
- 02 irony by understatement and overstatement: vocabulary-led (*no está mal* for "very good", *un poquito* for "a lot"), tagged with the unit vocabulary skill.
- 03 sarcastic questions and exclamations: recap `subjunctive-emotion` (A2) and exclamatives the sheet lists (*¡Qué…!*).
- 04 pretending: recap `como-si` (B2) at C1 density.
- 05 satire's targets: recap `rhetorical-devices` (B2); plus the text and the Reading group.
Domain words: humour (*la ironía, irónico, el sarcasmo, burlarse de, el chiste* only if not listed, *la exageración* only if not listed, *tomar el pelo*); check each against the sheet.
Text: a satirical column in an invented newspaper about a fictional office that announces a "meeting to reduce meetings"; irony must be clear from the text itself. No real person, company or institution.
