Genre: official report
Lesson plan:
- 01 NEW `relativos-el-cual`, screen `c1-07-01-a-gr`: *el cual, la cual, los cuales, las cuales, lo cual*. After a preposition, especially a long one (*en virtud del cual*, *a través de la cual*, *según el cual*), where *el que* is also correct but *el cual* is the formal choice; after a comma for a non-restrictive clause; *lo cual* for a whole previous clause (*No se presentó, lo cual retrasó el trámite*). Never *el cual* in a restrictive clause without a preposition (*el informe el cual leí* is wrong; *el informe que leí*). You know *el que* after prepositions from `relativas-preposicion-el-que` (B1). Why: it marks gender and number, so it clears up which noun is meant in long sentences. At least 6 items tagged with it.
- 02 possession in formal relatives: recap `relativo-posesivo-cuyo` (B1) at C1 density: *el ciudadano cuya solicitud…*, *cuyo plazo vence…*.
- 03 writing to an institution: recap `formal-register` (B2) and `imperfecto-de-cortesia` (B2): *Me dirijo a ustedes para…*, *Quería solicitarle…*, *Atentamente*.
- 04 procedures in the impersonal: recap `se-pasivo-vs-impersonal` (B2): *se ruega*, *se tramitarán las solicitudes*, *se atenderá a los usuarios*. Avoid *informar que* / *informar de que* entirely (its regime differs between regions).
- 05 bureaucratic style and plain language: recap `nominalizacion-despersonalizacion` (B2): rewrite *Se procedió a la realización de la revisión* as *Se revisó*; plus the text and the Reading group.
Domain words: administration and procedures (e.g. *el trámite, la solicitud, el plazo, la ventanilla, el expediente, tramitar, vencer, el formulario*); check each against the sheet first.
Text: an official report of an invented municipal office on a new online procedure for a common permit (what changes, what citizens must do, what happened in its first months, stated without real figures), written in formal administrative style with *el cual* and *cuyo* used naturally. No real municipality, law or official.
