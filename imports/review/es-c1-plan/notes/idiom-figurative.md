Genre: story
This unit teaches no new skill: lesson 01 is vocabulary-led (`c1-idiom-figurative-vocab`), and lessons 02–05 recap known B1/B2 skills at C1 density in idiom and figurative language (each recap screen opens with "You know *X* from <Level> Unit N", using the unit number from the knowledge sheet, and adds what is new at C1: how figurative and idiomatic Spanish uses the form). Items are tagged with those skills or `c1-idiom-figurative-vocab`.
Lesson plan:
- 01 Everyday figurative idioms and fixed expressions: vocabulary-led (`c1-idiom-figurative-vocab`), teaching how Spanish idioms pair concrete images with non-literal meanings (*meter la pata*, *dar en el clavo*, *tomar el pelo*, *echar una mano*, *quedarse de piedra*) and why idioms do not translate word for word.
- 02 Figurative prepositional verbs: recap `verbos-regimen-preposicional` (B1 Unit 29 taught literal change/experience verbs *acostumbrarse a, adaptarse a, empezar a, darse cuenta de*); new at C1: figurative verb+preposition idioms where the preposition changes the verb's sense (*dar con la clave*, *caer en la cuenta*, *cargar con la culpa*, *tropezar con un obstáculo*, *romper con la rutina*).
- 03 Proverbial and figurative manner frames: recap `manner-clauses` (B2 Unit 32 taught literal *como* / *según* + indicative vs subjunctive); new at C1: figurative and proverbial manner expressions (*como quien no quiere la cosa*, *según el dicho*, *como anillo al dedo*, *tal como suena*) alongside open vs known manner mood (*como mejor te parezca* vs *como dicta la costumbre*).
- 04 Emphatic fronting with figurative phrases: recap `inversion-enfasis-fronting` (B2 Unit 1 taught formal adjective and place fronting *Crucial fue la intervención*); new at C1: fronting idiomatic and adverbial phrases for narrative punch (*A ciegas caminaba el equipo*, *Sin rodeos respondió la directora*, *En el clavo dio aquel comentario*, *Ni una palabra soltó*), with subject-verb inversion when the subject is explicit.
- 05 Figurative gerunds in storytelling: recap `gerundio-acciones-paralelas` (B1 Unit 2 taught literal simultaneous physical actions *Llegó corriendo*); new at C1: figurative and idiomatic gerunds that show attitude or method (*andando con rodeos*, *midiendo sus palabras*, *haciendo la vista gorda*, *tirando la toalla*) while avoiding the *gerundio de posterioridad* for a later result; plus the story and the Reading group.
Domain words: idioms and figurative language (*el modismo, meter la pata, dar en el clavo, tomar el pelo, cargar, tropezar, encajar, el anillo, el dedo, figurado, literal, a ciegas, sin rodeos, el hilo, romper el hielo, tirar la toalla, hacer la vista gorda, esquivar, insinuar*); all checked against the knowledge sheet and word-lesson index.
Text: a short story in an invented setting (a small neighbourhood bookshop-café hosting a late-afternoon reading circle with Carlos and Meg) where an unexpected mix-up over a handwritten note unfolds through everyday idioms, figurative comparisons and narrative fronting; warm, witty, and grounded in ordinary detail. No real person, institution, study, statistic, or country.
