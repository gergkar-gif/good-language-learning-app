## Segment 02 (2012–2013)
- From 1 January 2012 the National Judicial Office (Országos Bírósági Hivatal, OBH) ran the central administration of the courts | seg 02, L02, cons | sure of the name and date. NB: Unit 25 writes "Országos Bírói Hivatal"; the official name is Országos Bírósági Hivatal (check Unit 25's text)
- Its president was elected by the Assembly for nine years | seg 02 | sure (verified in Unit 25)
- The first president was Handó Tünde | seg 02 | check (elected December 2011)
- The Assembly also elected the president of the Kúria | seg 02 | sure (verified in Unit 25)
- Judges were appointed by the President of the Republic on the proposal of the OBH president | seg 02, L02, ch | check
- The OBH president could designate another court for a case, i.e. transfer cases | seg 02, L02 | check
- The National Judicial Council (OBT) supervised the OBH president | seg 02, seg 03 | sure
- March 2012: the Venice Commission gave an opinion on the judicial laws and criticised the president's wide powers | seg 02 | sure (verified in Unit 25)
- July 2012: the Constitutional Court annulled the president's case-transfer power | seg 02, L02, ch | check (decision 33/2012)
- March 2013: the fourth amendment wrote this power into the Fundamental Law | seg 02 | check
- June 2013: the Venice Commission criticised the fourth amendment; the government said it was in line with European norms | seg 02, L02 cons | sure (verified in Unit 25)

## Segment 03 (2012–2019)
- The OBT replaced the National Council of Justice (Országos Igazságszolgáltatási Tanács) in 2012 | seg 03 | check
- Its members were elected by judges | seg 03 | sure
- Applications for court-leader posts were ranked by judicial bodies; the final decision lay with the OBH president | seg 03 | check
- The president declared several applications unsuccessful although the bodies had ranked the applicants | seg 03, L03, ch | check (several cases; no number given)

- Critics: it gave the minister of justice too large a role | seg 03 | check
- In December 2019 the Assembly repealed that law before it took effect | seg 03 | check
