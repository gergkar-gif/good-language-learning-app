Focus (B1 citizenship track; the user's rules, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-28). These four units are history units: the effort goes into the texts, not the grammar.

**Texts first.** Read every world segment (the story files `…-01-….json` to `…-05-….json`) closely, sentence by sentence: Hungarian that is wrong or unnatural, facts, dates, names, causes and results, and narration that contradicts another segment. Fixes go on the segment files; never on the combined unit reading `stories/world/b1/<unit stem>.json` (the reviewer rebuilds it). List every factual claim in `facts-<unit id>.md` (the history block below). A disputed matter is named in one line (*a történészek vitatják, …*, *egyes források szerint …*) rather than settled either way; a modern political judgement in a history text (who was right, what "saved the nation", praise or blame of a present-day party or government) is removed. Hedge traditions and legends (*a hagyomány szerint*, *a legenda szerint*).

**Reading questions** (the story files' `questions` and the lessons' `reading` items) must be answerable from the segments as written, and the lessons' reading items must not be copies of the story questions. Where the setup line says a story's questions are copies of another story's, write three new questions from that segment's own text.
