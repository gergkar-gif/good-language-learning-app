## Norm claims (exact wording from screens, for RAE verification)

1. `c1-09-01-a-gr.json` (text): "*Debido a*, *a raíz de* and *como consecuencia de* take a noun phrase or an infinitive."
2. `c1-09-01-a-gr.json` (text): "*Dado que* takes a conjugated verb in the indicative and introduces a fact the reader already accepts as the premise of the argument."
3. `c1-09-01-a-gr.json` (tip): "Watch the complement: *debido a*, *a raíz de* and *como consecuencia de* take a noun, never a bare conjugated verb (*debido a que* is needed before a clause). Conversely, *dado que* requires a conjugated verb in the indicative, never a bare noun or the subjunctive. Remember the contraction *a + el = al* (*debido al encarecimiento*)."
4. `c1-09-02-a-gr.json` (tip): "After *de*, always use an infinitive, never a conjugated verb: write *De no bajar las tasas*, never *De no bajan las tasas* or *De no bajaran las tasas*. Place *no* directly between *de* and the infinitive, and attach any reflexive or object pronoun to the infinitive (*de mantenerse*, *de haberlo previsto*). Set the opening phrase off with a comma."
5. `c1-09-03-a-gr.json` (tip): "Never put the conditional (*habría*) inside the *si* clause: write *si hubiera subido*, not *si habría subido*."
6. `c1-09-04-a-gr.json` (tip): "Both verbs must be in the subjunctive, never indicative: write *pase lo que pase* (not *pase lo que pasa*) and *sea cual sea* (not *sea cual es*). With *sea cual sea*, match the verb and *cual* to a plural noun: *sean cuales sean las consecuencias* is the standard plural form."
7. `c1-09-05-a-gr.json` (tip): "Watch the preposition: *acabar de* + infinitive (A2 Unit 32) means 'to have just done', whereas *acabar por* + infinitive means 'to end up doing'. Never drop *por* before the infinitive, and use these periphrases only when a prior process leads to the outcome."
8. `c1-09-05-b-gr.json` (tip): "*Pasar a* always requires the preposition *a* before the infinitive (*pasó a ser*, never *pasó ser* or *pasó de ser*). Remember that *pasar de* + infinitive is informal for 'not to bother doing' and does not mean 'to move on to'."
