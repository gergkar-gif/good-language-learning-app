So in Units 7–10 there is no preterite (*fue*, *compró*, *conocí*) and no imperfect: in Spain the *pretérito perfecto* goes with *hoy*, *esta semana*, *este año*, *ya*, *alguna vez*, *desde* / *desde hace*, and NOT with *ayer*, *anoche*, *la semana pasada*, *el año pasado*, *hace dos días* (those take the preterite): a perfecto sentence with such a marker is wrong, and a preterite is before its screen, so rewrite it with a perfecto marker. *desde el año pasado* + perfecto is fine. Unit 7 has about 49 past-time markers (*ayer*, *la semana pasada*): read every one.
