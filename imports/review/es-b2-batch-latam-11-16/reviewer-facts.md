## Read of the old texts (reviewer's knowledge unless a source is given)

**Pattern in this batch: invented witnesses.** Most texts stage named people (Don Baudilio, Doña Luz
Marina, Yeison, Don Bernardo, Juvencio Pemón, la doctora Arreaza, Coromoto, Marta Elena, el profesor
Arismendi…) who speak in quotation marks. These are generator inventions inside non-fiction: cut
every invented person and every invented quotation; keep the facts they carried only if true.
Worse, real people are given invented words or wrong roles: **Luz Marina Bernal** (a real Soacha
mother) gets an invented speech; **Carlos Castaño-Uribe** an invented quotation; **Diana Trujillo**
(a NASA engineer) appears as a biologist on a Chiribiquete flight. Cut all three.
