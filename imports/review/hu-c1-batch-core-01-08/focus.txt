**C1 generator habits to fix:** screen and lesson titles in inflated English ("Epistemic Speculative Modal Structures Exploring …"): make them plain ("Concession: *jóllehet*, *noha*, *ámbár*"); "C1" in screens, stems and goals ("In C1 formal Hungarian", "the most authentic C1 register") is a level label the learner doesn't need: say "formal", "in an essay"; question stems that ask which sentence is "most authentic" or "most sophisticated" test taste, not a form: ask about meaning or a single fault; wrong options with two or three faults or absurd content; English glosses starting a sentence with "Albeit" (not English). Academic vocabulary is the point of C1, but each sentence must be natural Hungarian and say something true; a calque or an empty sentence written to show a form is a defect (B2: *volt választásunk egymás iránt*).

**Core units:** rewrite every unmet word in items, options, prompts, screens and questions with met words (stories keep theirs; a screen's own new phrases and the lesson vocabulary list stay). Don't add skills. **Hints** name the base word and its role, never "X -> ending" or the answer form; a hint never equals the answer (give the English). **Second right answers** were the readers' commonest slip in B2: test each wrong option you write by asking whether a native would accept it (free word order; *noha* / *jóllehet* / *bár* are often interchangeable: put all that fit in `answers`, or make the item test a real difference). **Twin-form answers:** a fill-blank whose skill has two equal forms takes both in `answers`. A dialogue `prompt` fix is a list of turns (`[{"speaker": …, "text": …}]`), never the rendered "Speaker: text / …" string. A fix that changes `teaches` or `category` keeps the two matching. Check a phrase's government, not only its form. Use single quotes or *italics* for examples inside English text (double quotes break the JSON).

**Unit stories are texts under an author's name** (`c1-01-babits`, `c1-02-kosztolanyi`, `c1-03-szerb`, `c1-04-ady`, `c1-05-orkeny`, `c1-06-pilinszky`, `c1-07-eotvos`, `c1-08-mikszath`). Many are generator texts *about* a work or in its spirit, not the work itself. If a screen, goal, item or question says "In <work>, X does Y" or "<author> wrote that …", check it against the story as written and say "the text" when you can't confirm it is the author's; never present a generated sentence as a quotation. Report every claim about an author or work you doubt in group 4 with the segment id ("check"); the reviewer verifies them. Historical *vármegye* (Mikszáth's 19th-century county) stays; a present-day county is *megye* (since 1 October 2026). Flag every present-day claim (institutions, laws, figures, office names) "check".
