## For each story, check and fix

1. **Labels honest.** `title`, `author`, `work`, `source`: an original text has no real author; an adaptation names the real work; a text "in the manner of" an author says so ("Pastiche escrito para Parlour a la manera de …; no es un texto suyo.", as in `classics/b1/b1-21-paliques.json`). No invented author or work.
2. **Facts and plot of a real work.** Check every claim about a real author, work or event against the source. Project Gutenberg is reachable from this machine (`curl -sS -L https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/search/?query=<words>` to find an id, then `https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/<id>/pg<id>.txt`), and so is Spanish Wikisource (`python scripts/fetch_wikisource.py "<page title>" <out.txt>`). Save any source text you use under `/home/user/parlour-read-b/sources/` (create it). If the plot is wrong, correct it to the work; if you cannot find a source, keep to what you are sure of, say so in the report, and never quote from memory: no quotation marks around words attributed to an author unless you copied them from a source you read. Half-quotes (an author's line altered) are rewritten as plain retelling.
3. **Level: B1, no further.** Forms taught in B1 core are fine anywhere (the imperfect subjunctive after a past verb, reported speech, the present perfect subjunctive, *si hubiera … habría …*); forms beyond B1 are rewritten: *como si* + subjunctive, the *-se* imperfect subjunctive forms (*hubiese, tuviese*), the pluperfect subjunctive outside a *si* clause (*ojalá hubiera*), *antes de que / después de que*, rare literary words (*desaforados, inexorable*). Stories are exempt from the met-word rule (the learner taps unknown words), but not from the level. Keep length roughly as it is (rewrite, don't cut to a summary), the same paragraphs and events.
4. **Natural Spanish**, no template sentences spliced in from lessons, no sentence that repeats the one before, a coherent story from start to end.
5. **Comprehension questions** (`narration.pedagogical.comprehensionQuestions` or the file's own field): answerable from the text as written, in plain B1 Spanish (no *desopilante, suscitada* in a stem or option), each wrong option clearly wrong, `correctIndex` right, `explanation` true to the text. Rewrite any that ask about another text.
6. **Course facts:** if Carlos or Meg appear, the facts in the rulebook § 6c hold (Carlos is Hungarian, works in an office; Meg is South African, a student; they live in Budapest in B1; Ana and Andreas are Carlos's siblings; Lauren and Kaylee Meg's sisters). 7. **es-latam:** no *vosotros*/*os* forms anywhere (*ustedes*), Latin American words (*computadora, celular, carro, boleto, jugo, enojado*, *tomar* the bus; see `scripts/course_variants.py`); apart from those, the es-latam text says the same as the es-es one: apply your es-es fixes there too.
