## Norm claims

- `c1-26-01-a-gr`: "Never omit *de* in *el hecho de que* (writing *el hecho que* is *queísmo* and is rejected in formal prose), and keep the main verb singular to match *el hecho*: *El hecho de que existan varias becas ayuda a las familias* (not *ayudan*)."
- `c1-26-02-a-gr`: "Keep personal *haber de* + infinitive (*los estudiantes han de asistir*) distinct from impersonal *hay que* + infinitive (*hay que asistir*): *haber de* conjugates for person and number and names the specific academic actor responsible for the duty."
- `c1-26-03-a-gr`: "Never mix *se* passive with a *por* agent (*Se evaluó la tesis por el tribunal* is non-standard)."
- `c1-26-04-a-gr`: "Do not confuse *a propósito de* ('speaking of' + noun) with *a propósito* ('by the way' as a standalone opener, or 'on purpose' after a verb). When a noun follows, *de* is required: *A propósito de la tesis, ¿quién preside el tribunal?*"
- `c1-26-05-a-gr`: "In an absolute participle clause, the participle always precedes its noun subject and agrees with it in gender and number (*Publicados los resultados...*, *Concluida la ponencia...*). Never place a conjugated auxiliary inside the absolute clause."
