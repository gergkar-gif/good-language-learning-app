## What the read found (33 fixes)

- Unnatural or illogical collocations: an uptick that "exceeds the previous decline", *alcanzar
  un nuevo descenso*, a percentage share measured *en puntos*, *cuenta con aproximadamente dos
  personas*.
- Listening wrong options as unrelated multi-fault sentences with absolutes (*the whole
  population … in person*): a pattern the rulebook forbids; four items rewritten.
- A wrong grammar claim: *crecer* listed among irregular gerunds.
- A wrong option that is accepted Spanish (*en torno de*, in the DLE).
- Unpinned production prompts, goals no item practises (*la mitad*, *de* before a number),
  challenges whose scenario does not match the target, three category/tag mismatches.
- The article rule after *la mayoría de*: contested (common in use; the DPD rule cited by an
  academic source rejects it), so the items now test *la mitad de* + bare plural, which no one
  accepts.

Also, after planting: the c1-08-04 challenge target was redundant (*el treinta por ciento … en
torno a un tercio del total*) and used *total*, which es-es never lists; rewritten in both
courses.
