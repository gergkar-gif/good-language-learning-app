# Skill gap proposal (read-only analysis, 2026-10-07)

Scope: the gaps the user listed, checked against `skills/es.json`, `skills/hu.json`,
`skills/families.json`, `docs/skill-tagging-spec.md`, the grammar screens under
`content/<course>/grammar/` and the exercises under `content/<course>/exercises/`.
Nothing was edited. Counts are exercises whose `teaches` is a unit vocabulary skill
(`<level>-<unit id>-vocab`) and whose answer really depends on the pattern; pure
word glosses and `matching` items are excluded. Spanish counts are for `es-es`
(es-latam shares the exercise ids). Where a pattern turned out to be tagged on a
grammar skill rather than vocabulary, that is stated, because the retag then comes
from that skill instead.

Verdict key: ADD = new skill; MERGE = fits an existing skill; KEEP = stays unit vocabulary.
"Relevel" means lowering an existing B1 skill to A2 so A2 exercises may carry it (the
spec forbids tagging a skill above the exercise's level); that is a frozen-list change
and needs the user's sign-off, exactly like an ADD.

## Recommendation

The shortest list worth adding is four skills: `telling-time` (ES A1, about 26 exercises), `preguntas-indirectas` (ES A2, about 30, all retagged from `questions`), `reflexive-magam` (HU A2, 9) and `inkabb-mint` (HU A2, 16 including the 3 on `nek-conditional`), about 81 retags. Two more are defensible but thin and can be left out: `dates-months` (ES A1, 8) and `ide-oda-innen-onnan` (HU A2, exactly 6, all lifted from `itt-ott-here-there`), 14 retags, giving 95 for the full six. Two cases are better solved by relevelling an existing B1 skill to A2 than by adding one: `mediopassive-verbs-odik` (taught_in `a2-155-a-gr`, 6 retags from vocabulary) and `distributive-nkent` (taught_in `a2-68-b-gr`, 19 retags: 11 from vocabulary and 8 from `essive-formal-kent`); both need sign-off. The remaining MERGE items need no registry change, only retags (about 3 to `reflexives`, 2 to `demonstratives`, 1 to `pronombres-combinados`, 1 to `condicional-regular`, 10 to `temporal-postpositions`, 5 to `correlative-minel-annal`, 4 to `mediopassive-verbs-odik`; 26 retags), and everything else (weather, café, *presentar a*, directions, invitations, study life, -ás/-és, commemorative, alighogy, discourse particles, *az ember*, azelőtt/azóta) stays unit vocabulary. Total retagging: about 95 exercises for the six ADDs, plus 25 for the two relevels, plus 26 for the plain merges, roughly 146 exercises, of which the four-skill core accounts for about 81. Each ADD still needs a hand-checked sample and an entry in `skills/frozen-<lang>.json`; `telling-time`, `dates-months` and `preguntas-indirectas` also change the ES registry, so es-es and es-latam retag together. Two side findings: `a1-04-02.ex03` (*presenta __ Meg*) really tests `a-personal` but is tagged vocabulary, and the unit `asking-what-happened` currently puts a whole reported-speech pattern on an A1 skill about question words.
