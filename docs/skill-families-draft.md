# Vocabulary topic families — draft for approval (ROADMAP 125, step 1)

Status: **draft, 2026-10-04. Not enforced and not in `skill-registry.json` yet.**
Decided 2026-10-04: keep all five added families (21–25), and allow up to
two families per unit, the main one first. One more family (26, `ideas`)
is proposed below and still needs a decision. The unit-by-unit mapping is in
[skill-families-mapping.md](skill-families-mapping.md).
Once approved, each unit vocabulary skill (`a1-unit05-vocab`,
`b1-orszagma-vocab`, …) gets one or more of these as its `family` in the
registry. Grammar families are a separate list, still to be drafted.

## Sources checked

| Source | What it is | Why it counts |
|---|---|---|
| **CEFR (2001), §4.1, themes of communication** | 14 themes taken from the Threshold Level (1990). The CEFR gives them as an example classification, not a required list. | It's the framework itself. Short and coarse. |
| **Plan Curricular del Instituto Cervantes (PCIC), *Nociones específicas*** | 20 thematic notion areas, each itemised A1–C2. The PCIC sets them against the CEFR's personal, public, occupational and educational domains. | The CEFR-based reference for Spanish, used by DELE/SIELE. The most detailed of the three, and it covers every level. |
| **Hungarian érettségi, Hungarian as a foreign language, oral topics** | 10 topic areas for the school-leaving exam (intermediate ≈ B1, advanced ≈ B2). | The only official Hungarian-as-a-foreign-language topic list that could be retrieved. The ECL and Origó topic lists aren't published online (both exam centres' PDFs returned 404). |

The PCIC list is the backbone because it covers all levels and is the
most detailed. The other two are used to check for anything it leaves out.

**Decided (2026-10-04):** keep all five added families, and allow up to two
families per unit, the main one first.

**Still to decide: family 26, `ideas`.** Mapping every unit turned up 51
vocabulary skills that don't belong to any topic: *Making Decisions*
(option, criterion, advantage), *Possibilities & Predictions* (likely,
uncertainty, hypothesis), *Complex Opinions*, and most of the es-latam B2
units named after a grammar point. Their words are what PCIC lists under
*Nociones generales*, its non-topic vocabulary. The options are a 26th
family `ideas`, or to fold them into `language`, which would then mean
"language, argument and reasoning". My recommendation: keep `ideas`
separate. Folding them in would make `language` the biggest family by far,
and "practise your reasoning words" is a different thing for a learner than
"practise your language-learning words".

## What the cross-check shows about our units

This section came first, from reading every unit title in all three courses (637 units). The unit-by-unit mapping below confirms it.

1. **Well covered at A1–B1:** families 3–8, 10, 12–14 and 20–22 all have
   units at every level. The shared B1 core list (*Relationships*,
   *Work & Professional Life*, *Health & Wellbeing*, …) lines up almost
   one-to-one with the PCIC areas.
2. **Gaps against the standards:**
   - **`religion-philosophy` (PCIC 19).** No Spanish unit at any level.
     In Hungarian it appears only from B2 (*Political Philosophy*) and C1.
     PCIC itemises this area from A1, at a basic level (festivals,
     beliefs).
   - **`services` (PCIC 11, CEFR "services").** Hungarian A2 has *Post
     Office*, *Banking*, *Pharmacy*. Spanish Core has no services unit at
     any level. Only the es-es citizenship track touches it
     (*Consumo… Servicios Bancarios*, *Sistema Nacional de Salud*).
   - **Clothes (inside `shopping`).** Hungarian A1 has *Clothes &
     Appearance*. Spanish A1/A2 have no clothes unit.
   - **`education` at A1.** Spanish meets it only at A2 (*Studying and
     School Life*). Hungarian has it at A2 and B1.
3. **Units named after a function or grammar point, not a topic.** Most
   Spanish A2 units and the es-latam B2 grammar units were mapped from their
   vocabulary lists, not their titles. Most of them turned out to be topical
   anyway (A2 *Following Instructions* is cooking, *Talking About Experiences*
   is the coast). The rest are the 51 `ideas` skills above.
4. **The topic list is set by level in the standards, not just by name.**
   PCIC assigns each item a level, and *Economía e industria* at A2 is
   prices and shops, not fiscal policy. The family says *what area* a word
   belongs to, and the level still comes from the unit. That's
   consistent with how the registry already stores `level`.

## What the mapping found (2026-10-04)

Every unit vocabulary skill is now mapped: 634 in all (HU 290, es-es 111,
es-latam 233), plus 3 excluded. The full table is in
[skill-families-mapping.md](skill-families-mapping.md) and the data in
[skill-families-mapping.json](skill-families-mapping.json). The mapping
confirms the gaps in finding 2. Shopping also turns out to have no unit at
B1 or above in any course. The mapping also surfaced problems with the
vocabulary skills themselves, which are now in ROADMAP item 125:

- **`a1-unit01-vocab` (A1 greetings) is a catch-all in both Spanish
  courses.** It sits on 533 exercises per course, and 520 of them are
  outside unit 1: the A1 café, weather, work, hobbies and health units, and
  the A2 grammar units. It was put there by `2015b0a8` (2026-09-25, "Split
  Spanish junk-drawer grammar skills"). Spanish has only 12 vocabulary skills
  for 26 A1 units, and none for A2 units 21–29, so the split had nothing
  better to point those exercises at.
- **Mixed granularity in es-latam B2.** 60 skills are per lesson (`b2-31-01-vocab` …
  `futuro-05-vocab`, each used by one exercise) where every other unit has
  one skill per unit. Merging them is a skill-list change, so it needs
  sign-off under the freeze.
- **Registry errors:** `hacer` (both Spanish courses) and `negation`
  (es-es) are registered as `kind: vocabulary` but aren't unit themes.
  Hungarian has two slugs for one unit, `c1-pesti-ironia-vocab` and
  `c1-pestiironia-vocab`. es-latam's `a2-unit33-vocab` (the es-es-only
  *vosotros* unit) is registered but unused.
- **Orphan lessons:** `a1-18-01` and `a1-18-02` exist in both Spanish
  courses but aren't wired into any unit. A1 unit 18, *Work & Obligations*,
  uses `a1-work-*`.
- **Not an error after all:** Hungarian A2 slugs are numbered unit + 10
  (`a2-unit11-vocab` = A2 unit 1). So the `a2-unit01-vocab` →
  `a2-unit11-vocab` move in `e7f4c105` was a renumbering, not a wrong-unit
  retag.
